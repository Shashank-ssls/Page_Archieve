# PRODUCT.md - what we are selling and why

## Promise
Save anything on the web exactly as you saw it. Private, searchable, share-safe.

## Market position (research from 2026-10-22)
- Leader: GoFullPage, 11M users, 4.9 stars. Simple, trusted, paid editor.
  Briefly pulled from the Chrome Web Store in Aug 2026 over a copyright dispute;
  its users had nowhere obvious to go.
- Niche tools exist for chat export, evidence capture, breakpoints, redaction and
  web-to-video, but almost all have under 200 users. The opening is capture
  quality plus distribution, not a never-seen feature.
- Our moat: the engine knows the exact position of every word, link and image
  on a fully rendered page. Every Pro feature is built on that.

## Buyers, in order
1. AI power users saving, citing and sharing long ChatGPT/Claude threads.
2. Designers, QA and marketers who need pixel-accurate captures of modern sites.
3. Later: freelancers and small teams who need a dated, hashed record of a page.

rule, so only Lite is public.

## Free vs Pro
| Feature | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-page capture, chat presets, auto-expand | Yes | Yes |
| PNG, JPEG, image PDF, naming templates, folders | Yes | Yes |
| Searchable PDF text layer | Yes | Yes |
| Living PDF (links, bookmarks, cover) | - | Yes |
| Share-safe Redact | - | Yes |
| Scroll Reel | With watermark | No watermark |
| Capture Library (later) | - | Yes |
| Breakpoint Board (later) | - | Yes |
| Proof Stamp (later) | - | Yes |
Rule: Free must already beat the leader on hard pages. That earns the reviews.

## Store and legal rules
- No competitor names in the listing or UI; comparisons only on our own site.
- One clear single purpose: capturing web pages.
- Privacy policy must list every network call (license check; later the
  timestamp hash). Nothing else leaves the device.
- Measure with the store dashboard and sales data only. No analytics.

## Distribution
1. Before/after clip of a long chat (owner posts).
2. Watermarked free Scroll Reels shared on social.
3. Store search terms: "full page screenshot", "save ChatGPT conversation as
   PDF", "Claude chat export". Also publish on Edge Add-ons.
4. Comparison and how-to pages on the landing site.
5. Launches: Product Hunt, Show HN, Indie Hackers.
6. One-time rating prompt after the third successful capture.
